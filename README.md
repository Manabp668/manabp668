## 🚀 My Startups

### 🚗 Orzyy — Smart Vehicle Safety

> Protect your vehicle with smart QR emergency alerts.

Register your vehicle, generate a unique QR sticker, and get instant notifications whenever someone scans it — without ever displaying your phone number publicly. Built for emergencies and parking situations across India.

**Stack:** Flutter · PHP/MySQL · Firebase OTP · QR Code system

[![Play Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google_Play-Orzyy-414141?style=flat-square&logo=google-play&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=orzyy.manab)

### 🤝 Hohay — Community Help

> Community help alerts, blood donation & emergency response near you.

A location-based community platform for Assam — post emergency alerts, find blood donors, and coordinate help with people around you in real time.

**Stack:** Flutter · PHP/MySQL · Firebase · GPS

[![Play Store](https://img.shields.io/badge/Google_Play-Hohay-414141?style=flat-square&logo=google-play&logoColor=white)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.protim.hohay)
